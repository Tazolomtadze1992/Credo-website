What the evidence tells us

1997 is the origin, 2017 is the bank.

The story starts with World Vision’s enterprise-development programme. Independent registration follows in 2005 and the MFO company in December 2007. The National Bank grants the banking licence on 20 March 2017.

The regional story has real photographs.

Reports contain old orange-and-blue branches, early interiors, teams and customers. These are stronger timeline material than generic office imagery. The 2013 report explicitly documents ten new branches.

Digital banking is a progression.

Accounts, deposits and debit cards in 2018; Mycredo in 2019; online onboarding and contracts in 2020; business banking and phone-based card acceptance in 2022. Avoid presenting all digital products as one launch.

Further milestones to consider

  • 2007: BlueOrchard’s financing relationship begins. Credo’s 2009 report and BlueOrchard’s later partnership comments both trace it back to this period.
  • 2016: FMO’s first USD 10m facility and capacity-development support. FMO’s retrospective ↗
  • 2021: Credo joins the Global Alliance for Banking on Values. The alliance’s own 2021 review is the clearest joining-year evidence; its 2024 European gathering is a separate event.
  • October 2022: first dedicated expat service centre opens in Tbilisi, at 16 Chavchavadze Avenue. GABV coverage ↗
  • 2024: Stepantsminda branch opening has real photographs in Credo’s media archive. The official 2024 history also records new regional and urban service centres.
  • 2025: GEL 80m agreement with FMO, refreshed Mycredo and the “AGROVE” savings feature are recorded in Credo’s official year-end history. Treat the year-end branch/customer figures as dated snapshots.
  • 27 October 2026: Credo announces USD 10m of privately placed AT1 bonds with TBC Capital. Official announcement ↗
